Who is this for?

Designed for elderly people who need assistance with daily technology issues.

Why a Human Helper?

We believe that AI alone isn't enough. A trusted human helper is needed and valued.

What's the value for the Human Helper?

Many people already spend many hours helping elderly parents or friends. Teki provides both notifications and transcripts of chats to reduce the number of needed calls or length of time required to assist the elderly user. Teki really helps BOTH parties.
