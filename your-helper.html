Your Helper

Choose a family member, friend, and someone you trust to be your helper. Both of you will have access to Teki.

For You

Invite your chosen helper to engage with Teki. Ask for help from your helper when you want it.

For Your Helper

Accept the invitation to be a helper, create an account with Teki, and review interactions between Teki and the person you are a helper to.

A connection you control

  1. Invite your trusted Helper

  2. Your Helper is added to your account

  3. Your Helper reviews your interactions with Teki

Your privacy comes first

  • Sharing always needs your explicit consent.
  • You can disconnect your helper at any time.
  • Your full chat history is never shared automatically.
  • This is a chosen-person connection, not on-demand or instant support.
