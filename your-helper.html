A familiar person, when you need one

Teki is always here to guide you, but sometimes it helps to have a familiar person step in. You choose who that person is.

For the person getting help

Invite someone you trust to be your helper. When you want extra support, you can reach them without leaving Teki.

For the helper

Accept an invitation to support someone you care about. You only see what they choose to share, and only when they ask.

A connection you control

  1. Invite your helper

    Send an invitation to the person you want to connect with.

  2. They accept

    Your helper confirms the connection from their own device.

  3. Share only what you choose

    You decide what to share, each time, with explicit consent.

  4. Disconnect any time

    You can end the connection whenever you like, no reason needed.

Your privacy comes first

  • Sharing always needs your explicit consent.
  • You can disconnect your helper at any time.
  • Your full chat history is never shared automatically.
  • This is a chosen-person connection, not on-demand or instant support.
