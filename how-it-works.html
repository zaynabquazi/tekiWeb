SIMPLE STEPS

Get help at your own pace

Teki breaks technology help into small, clear steps so you never feel rushed. Take each one when you are ready.

  1. Tell Teki about the problem you are having

    Use your own words. For example, "Teki, my printer isn't working."

  2. Teki will answer your questions and display the answers on your screen

    The display window shows you what Teki's response is. You can ask follow up questions.

  3. Include your helper in the conversation with Teki

    Adding your helper keeps you connected with the person you trust.

Why we are building Teki

Too many people feel left behind by technology. Teki exists to make everyday technology feel approachable, so more people can stay connected and confident.

What makes Teki different

An approachable animated guide

A friendly, animated helper makes getting started feel calm and welcoming.

You set the pace

Controls let you slow down, pause, and repeat so you stay in charge.

A private, chosen-person connection

You decide who can help you, and that connection stays private to you.
